Chatterbox TTS supports bracketed speech tags.

Emotion tags: [angry], [fear], [surprised], [whispering], [advertisement], [dramatic], [narration], [crying], [happy], [sarcastic].
Sound effect tags: [clear throat], [sigh], [shush], [cough], [groan], [sniff], [gasp], [chuckle], [laugh].

Use tags sparingly and only when the words justify the performance: for example [sigh] before an apology or failure, [happy] for relief after success, [chuckle] or [laugh] only when something is actually funny, and [dramatic] for real stakes.

Put a tag immediately before the sentence or phrase it colors.

Use at most one tag in a terse update and at most two tags in a longer spoken answer. Do not invent tags, and do not tag neutral status text.
